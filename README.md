# Ami Tracker

Ami Tracker allows you to be up-to-date with any changes on [amiami's website](https://www.amiami.com). Amiami's catalog
is huge and stock/price changes happen all the time, so instead of refreshing the site by hand, you tell the bot what
you care about and it lets you know when something changes!

**Invite the bot and use the /wizard command to get started!**

### Product Search Notifications

**TL;DR** sends you notification when product becomes available.

Allows you to keep track of specific search queries, what products are there and the product's availability.

### Wishlist

**TL;DR** private wishlists that send notifications into your DMs.

Allows you to keep track of specific products that you wish to buy. The notifications are sent directly into your DMs.

### Inventory

Allows you to track what you have bought on amiami. Currently just a random feature that I wanted to add; later you will
be able to see how many figures, plushies etc. you've bough on amiami and how much you've spent.

### Embed support

Ami Tracker automatically recognizes amiami's URLs in Discord messages and sends the corresponding product info.

### Good to know

- The free tier comes with some default limits (right now: 1 wishlist, 10 entries per wishlist, 5 search notifications
  per server) to keep things running smoothly for everyone. If you need more, join the support server and ask, limits
  get raised by hand, no payment required.
- The bot only reads data that's already public on AmiAmi's website. It doesn't read your regular server messages, and
  there's no in-bot purchase flow or payment info being collected.
- Data is stored so wishlists, notifications and settings survive restarts and persist even if the bot is removed from
  your server. You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time, see the Privacy Policy below for how.
