## 1. About the Bot

The Bot is a third-party tool that helps users track product listings on [amiami](https://www.amiami.com/) (a Japanese hobby/figure retailer), including:

- Looking up product information (`/product`).
- Creating and managing personal wishlists, and adding/removing products to/from them (`/wishlist`).
- Tracking products you have already bought, including purchase price and date (`/inventory`).
- Creating channel-based notifications for new product listings, stock changes, and price changes (`/search-notification`).
