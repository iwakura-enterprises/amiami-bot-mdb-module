# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-07-18**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord bot "Ami Tracker" (the "Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use it. It supplements the [Terms of Service](TOS.md) and should be read together with it. Where this Policy uses terms like "Guild," "Channel," or "User," they have the same meaning as in the Terms of Service.

If you do not agree with this Policy, do not add or use the Bot.

## 1. Data Controller

The Bot is developed and operated by an individual, contactable at:

**mayuna@iwakura.enterprises**

For the purposes of applicable data protection law (including the GDPR), this contact acts as the data controller for personal data processed by the Bot.

## 2. What Personal Data We Collect

The Bot collects and stores the following categories of personal data, indefinitely, as described in the Terms of Service:

| Category | Data | Collected when |
|---|---|---|
| Guild data | Discord guild ID, guild name, preferred/secondary currencies, guild-level usage limits | The Bot is added to a server or its settings are configured |
| Channel data | Discord channel ID, channel name, parent guild, notification configuration | A channel is configured for product notifications |
| User data | Discord user ID, username, preferred currency, personal settings, user-level usage limits | You interact with any Bot command |
| Wishlist data | Wishlist names, settings, and the products added to them | You create or edit a wishlist |
| Purchase data | Products you mark as "bought," including price paid and purchase date | You use the inventory/bought-products feature |

We do **not** collect: message content outside of direct command interactions, payment card or bank details (see Section 6 of the Terms of Service regarding paid upgrades), email addresses, IP addresses, or any data beyond what is needed for the features listed above.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above solely to operate and provide the Bot's features, specifically to:

- Identify and address notifications to the correct Guild, Channel, or User.
- Persist your wishlists, tracked purchases, and settings between sessions.
- Apply currency preferences to displayed product information.
- Enforce per-user and per-guild usage limits.
- Diagnose and fix technical errors (see Section 5, Sentry).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the Bot. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

- **Performance of a contract / at your request** (Art. 6(1)(b)): storing your wishlists, settings, and purchase records, which you actively create and which the Bot's core functionality depends on.
- **Legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)): storing Guild/Channel configuration data and technical error data (via Sentry, see Section 5) to operate, maintain, and improve the Bot reliably.

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests; see Section 8 for your rights.

## 5. Third Parties and Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited data is shared with the following service providers strictly to operate the Bot:

- **Discord**: The Bot necessarily operates through Discord's platform and API. Discord processes messages, commands, and interactions independently under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). We do not control Discord's own data practices.
- **Sentry**: We use Sentry for error monitoring and diagnostics. Sentry receives only technical error data (e.g., stack traces, exception details) generated when the Bot encounters a bug or failure. **Sentry does not receive personally identifiable information such as Discord user IDs, usernames, wishlist contents, or purchase data.**
- **AmiAmi (amiami.com)**: The Bot retrieves publicly available product data from AmiAmi's website to provide its core functionality. No personal data about you is sent to AmiAmi.
- **Hosting/database infrastructure**: The Bot's data is stored on infrastructure operated or contracted by us to run the Bot. No other third party has access to this data beyond what is necessary for hosting and operation.

Where any of these third-party providers process data outside the European Economic Area, they do so under that provider's own applicable safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses), to the extent applicable.

We may also disclose data if required to do so by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

## 6. Data Retention

Personal data is retained **indefinitely**, for as long as needed to provide the Bot's functionality. Removing the Bot from a Guild, or no longer using a command, does **not** automatically delete previously stored data — data remains stored until a deletion request is made (see Section 7).

- **Rectify** inaccurate data.
- **Erase** ("right to be forgotten") your data.
- **Restrict or object** to certain processing.
- **Data portability**, where technically feasible.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection supervisory authority.
